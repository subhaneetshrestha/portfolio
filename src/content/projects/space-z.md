# space-z

A 2D vertical-ascent roguelite for phones and PC. One rocket climbs from
Earth through the solar system to the Void, fighting swarms and drafting
power-ups. One-thumb controls and auto-fire on the surface; ARPG-style
build synergies and weapon evolutions underneath.

Everything is generated in code. The neon-vector art has no external
assets; the audio is synthesized — per-zone generative chiptune and
procedural SFX. Seven zones, each ending in a unique boss. Eleven
weapons, eight evolutions, eleven passives, a Star-Map tech tree, eight
ships, daily seeds, and a hidden hardcore mode unlocked by a flawless run.

Every push to main runs the tests, builds Android, Windows and a
universal .love, bumps the version and publishes a release.

status: content-complete; balancing and polish.
stack: Lua, LÖVE 11
